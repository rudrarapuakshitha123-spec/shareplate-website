About Us

A volunteer-led NGO tackling hunger and food waste together.

Every day, tonnes of edible food are thrown away while families go hungry. SharePlate Foundation was started by students and volunteers to close that gap with simple coordination and technology.

Mission: make sure no surplus meal goes to waste when someone nearby needs it.
Vision: a zero-hunger, zero-waste community.

How it works

  1. Donor posts surplus food details
  2. Nearby volunteers get notified
  3. Food is collected safely
  4. Meals are delivered to communities
